PRINCIPLES

The standards behind the work.

SEVEN STANDARDS
APPLIED IN THIS ORDER, ON EVERY PROJECT
01 Understand before designing The visual direction should follow the business, audience, offer and desired outcome.
02 Structure before decoration A strong website begins with hierarchy, messaging and a clear visitor journey.
03 Design with purpose Every visual decision should strengthen perception, understanding, usability or conversion.
04 Build responsibly The implementation should remain maintainable, responsive, performant and appropriate to the project.
05 Protect the foundation Security should be considered during the build, not added only after a problem occurs.
06 Communicate clearly Clients should understand the project, priorities, decisions and next steps.
07 Stay involved The strategic thinking should remain connected to the final execution.
