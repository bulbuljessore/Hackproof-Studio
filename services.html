Before we begin.

ASKED FIRST, ANSWERED PLAINLY
Q

Do you design completely new websites?

Yes. Custom websites are designed and developed around the business, its audience, its content and what the site has to achieve commercially.

Q

Can you redesign an existing website?

Often. We start by auditing what you have, keep the parts that still earn their place, and rebuild the rest around a clearer structure.

Q

Is development included?

Design and build run as one connected process where development sits inside the project scope, so the site is made by the same hands that designed it.

Q

Is website security included?

Secure development is part of every build: access, updates, backups, and hardening are set up before launch. Audits, recovery, monitoring and specialist protection are scoped separately.

Q

Can you work with our existing content?

Existing content can be reviewed and reorganised. Where the current material no longer supports the business, the project may need rewriting or new content.

Q

How long does a website project take?

It depends on scope and on how quickly content and feedback come back. You get a dated schedule before design starts, and it holds unless the scope changes.

Q

What happens after launch?

You get a handover you can act on, and the option of ongoing monitoring and support. Nothing is locked to us. To begin, send an Open Brief and you will get a written reply.
