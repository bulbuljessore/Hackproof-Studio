Before we begin

ASKED FIRST, ANSWERED PLAINLY
Q

How long does a website project take?

It depends on scope and on how quickly content and feedback come back. You get a dated schedule before design starts, and it holds unless the scope changes.

Q

Do you redesign existing websites?

Often. We start by auditing what you have, keep the parts that still earn their place, and rebuild the rest around a clearer structure.

Q

Is website security included?

Secure development is part of every build: access, updates, backups, and hardening are set up before launch. Ongoing monitoring is arranged separately.

Q

What happens after launch?

You get a handover you can act on, and the option of ongoing monitoring and support. Nothing is locked to us.
